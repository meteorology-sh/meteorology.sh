The readout

  • Labels are lowercase and spelled out in full.
  • Values are mono. Units follow in ink-faint.
  • The first value is set in accent.
  • Wind reads the way a METAR carries it: three digits, nearest ten degrees.
  • Before data arrives, each value shows an em dash and the status reads WAITING.

Voice

HOW WE WRITE
  1. Prefer the simple, short explanation with minimal jargon.
  2. Use direct, affirmative sentences.
  3. Skip sentences that set up an idea and then negate or expand it.
  4. Skip hedging and equivocation.

On brand

  • A cloud is seeded by various materials in the form of crystalline dust, which nucleate supercooled liquid water and precipitate from the sky.
  • It delivers a 1.5 kg reagent to 18,000 ft in a 40 minute mission.
  • Weatherman collates metadata from a combination of NOAA data sources, including national radar mosaics, satellites, and advanced models.

Off brand

  • Petrichor is revolutionising the future of weather.
  • This is more than weather software. It is a new way to think about rain.
  • Seeding may, in some conditions, potentially help increase rainfall.
  • CAPE 1,250 · FZL 4.5 kft

Don'ts

THREE
  1. Do not set primary as text. It reads at 2.8:1 on ground. Use accent.
  2. Do not add a sixth colour. Name a state with a word, like LIVE or WAITING.
  3. Do not round a panel. Only status pills take a radius.
