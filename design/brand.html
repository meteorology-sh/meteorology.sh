Positioning

ONE SENTENCE

For Texas water districts and ranchers, Petrichor is the rain enhancement laboratory that measures every mission and publishes the data.

Every decision on this board serves that sentence. The brand has to read as an instrument a hydrologist will trust and a rancher will understand.

Direction

THREE CONSIDERED, ONE CHOSEN

Chosen

Instrument-grade restraint

True black, dense telemetry type, monospace numerals. Reads as a research lab that ships software. Matches the console the company already built.

Considered

Texas frontier hardware

Derrick silhouettes, gold foil, WPA poster weight. Strong for a hardware outfit. It undersells the software, which is the actual product.

Considered

Editorial science

Serif, wide measures, journal layout. Good for the research series. It looks like a publisher rather than an operator.

Voice

HOW WE WRITE

On brand

  • Across 41 randomised cases over the Edwards Plateau, seeded cells produced a mean rain volume 14% above control (95% CI: 2–27%).
  • Cleared for launch. Seeding band 19,140–26,729 ft MSL. Window closes 04:12Z.
  • We fly the clouds that make rain.

Off brand

  • Petrichor is revolutionising the future of weather.
  • Our AI-powered platform unlocks unprecedented rainfall.
  • Seeding works. (no number, no interval, no source)

Lead with the measurement. Name the uncertainty in the same sentence as the result. Write short, affirmative sentences. Full rules live in .claude/skills/voice/SKILL.md.

Don'ts

THREE
  1. Do not set primary as text. It reads at 2.8:1 on ground. Use accent.
  2. Do not add a sixth colour. Status belongs in a word, not a hue.
  3. Do not round a panel. Only status pills take a radius.
